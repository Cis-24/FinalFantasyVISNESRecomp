# r28: dances, rages, Magitek and items for the animation script gap (PLAN phase 8). Six battles against the two
# Narshe guards, entered through the battle event at $7E:1FF7 (group 1) like r16 to r27, each with the battle
# background set in the event (4D 01 <bg>: the low six bits of the second byte, $3F = map default, field/event.asm
# EventCmd_4d) to the one whose dance is used (data/battle_bg_dance.asm), so the dance command neither changes the
# background nor stumbles (battle/battle_cmd.asm, command $13): Forest Suite (FOREST_WOB, Harvester), Desert Aria
# (DESERT_WOB, Antlion), Love Sonata (map default NARSHE_EXT, Specter and Tapir), Earth Blues (MOUNTAINS_EXT, Land
# Slide), Dusk Requiem (CAVES, Cave In and Snare), Snowman Jazz (SNOWFIELDS, Snowball). A dancer goes on dancing at
# his own ATB pace (battle/action_1.asm RandDanceAction; steps drawn 7/16, 3/8, 1/8, 1/16). Vicks dances in every
# battle; Wedge dances Love Sonata with him in battle 3 (Tapir is the 1/16 step) and in the other battles uses Rage
# with the one rage known there ($1D2C), then acts on his own (attack two of the rage in half of his turns,
# battle/action_1.asm RandRage, data/monster_rage.asm): Reach Frog (Slimer), Rider (Virite), Sky Cap (Megazerk),
# Reach Frog again, Lethal Wpn (Grav Bomb). Terra acts once per battle: Magitek Bio Blast, Confuser and X-Fer (her
# graphics id selects TerraMagitekAttackTbl, btlgfx/cursor.asm @8625), then the items Revivify, Eyedrop and Remedy.
# Terra's ATB is forced full before each battle so that her menu comes first, and held at 0 once it is open. Wedge's
# and Vicks' menus follow in the order their gauges fill; both take the same input (A on the command, the list entry
# and the target), since the dance list cursors are set by poke while the list is open (the menu resets them when it
# opens). In drafts, setting Wedge's or Vicks' gauge to 0 by poke at the battle start left that member without a menu
# for the rest of the battle ($3AA0.1 set, .7 clear; battle/action_1.asm CheckPlayerAction), so their gauges are never
# touched. The guards
# are held at ATB 0, their HP is refreshed and they are immune to the dead and petrify status (ImmuneStatus1, $331C +
# target), so the instant death attacks cannot end a battle early. A battle ends with the dead status poked on the
# guards, seen by CheckBattleEnd after the next action. A wait of 30 frames precedes each battle start (see
# docs/STATUS.md, "r17 battle start phase").
