# r27: monster attacks with special effects that no route reached before (PLAN phase 8): Escape, Launcher, Fallen One,
# Evil Toot, Engulf and Bababreath. Six battles (the Dobermans first, since only in a later battle the whole party
# starts with a full gauge and can fight) entered through the battle event at $7E:1FF7 (group 1, battle type forced to
# normal before the event); the formation the event draws into $11E0 is replaced by `pokefor 11E0` before the battle
# init reads it (field/event.asm EventBattle, battle/init_2.asm LoadBattleProp), so formations without an event battle
# group can be fought: Doberman x2 (46; one is killed by poke, the other runs with Escape when it is alone), Proto
# Armor (402; alone it draws Launcher, Schiller or Tek Laser), Doom Drgn (300; Fallen One, one in three, as the
# counter to each of the party's three Fights), Critic and three Pan Doras (315; Evil Toot in their first and third
# turn), Zone Eater (501; Engulf, a short window so that not everyone is engulfed) and Phunbaba 3 (422; HP below
# 15360, so the first Fight draws Bababreath twice and ends the battle). Bababreath removes its targets from the party
# after the battle (battle_end.asm UpdateSRAM, as for Possess), so Phunbaba comes last. The party's ATB is held at 0
# once the first menu is open; where the party has to hit, A is pressed so the open menu fights once. Party HP is 9999
# and its status is cleared once a second. Battles that the monsters do not end are ended with the dead status poked
# on them. A wait of 30 frames precedes each battle start (see docs/STATUS.md, "r17 battle start phase").
